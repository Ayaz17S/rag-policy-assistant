# New Joiner Onboarding FAQ — Nimbus Telecom (Fictional)

## First Day
Report to the reception at 9:30 AM with your offer letter, a government photo ID and two passport-size photographs. Your laptop and ID card are handed over by the IT and admin teams on day one.

## Payroll and Bank Account
Salary is credited on the last working day of every month. Submit your bank account details and PAN on the HR portal within the first week so that your first salary is not delayed.

## Probation
The probation period is 6 months. Confirmation depends on the manager's review and completion of mandatory trainings.

## Mandatory Trainings
All new joiners must finish Information Security Awareness, Code of Conduct, and Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH) trainings on the learning portal within 30 days of joining.

## Health Insurance
Group health insurance of INR 5 lakh covers the employee, spouse and up to two children from the date of joining. Parents can be added at the employee's own cost during the annual enrolment window in April.

## Who to Contact
For HR queries email hr-help@nimbus.example. For laptop or access issues raise a ticket on the IT helpdesk portal or call extension 4357.
