# Travel and Expense Reimbursement Policy — Nimbus Telecom (Fictional)

## Booking Travel
All business travel must be booked through the company travel desk at least 10 days in advance. Economy class air travel is allowed for flights under 6 hours; employees at band 6 and above may book business class for flights longer than 6 hours.

## Hotel Limits
Hotel stay is reimbursed up to INR 6,000 per night in metro cities and up to INR 4,000 per night in other cities. Stays above the limit need written approval from the department head.

## Daily Allowance
A daily allowance of INR 1,500 covers meals and local travel for domestic trips. For international trips the daily allowance is USD 60.

## Submitting Claims
Expense claims must be submitted on the finance portal within 30 days of returning from the trip, with original bills attached. Claims submitted after 60 days are rejected. Approved claims are paid with the next monthly salary.

## Non-Reimbursable Items
Alcohol, personal entertainment, traffic fines, and minibar charges are not reimbursed.
