# Hybrid Work and Work From Home Policy — Nimbus Telecom (Fictional)

## Hybrid Schedule
Employees follow a hybrid model of 3 days in office and 2 days remote each week. Teams choose their common office days, which the manager publishes at the start of each quarter.

## Eligibility
New joiners must work from office full-time for the first 30 days for onboarding. Employees on a performance improvement plan are not eligible for remote days.

## Equipment and Internet
The company provides a laptop, headset and a one-time home-office allowance of INR 5,000. An internet reimbursement of up to INR 1,000 per month is paid against a valid bill.

## Working Hours
Core collaboration hours are 11 AM to 4 PM IST, during which employees must be reachable on Teams. Remote employees must connect through the company VPN for all internal systems.

## Working From Another City
Working remotely from a different city for more than 2 weeks requires manager and HR approval. Working from outside India is not permitted due to data protection and tax rules.
