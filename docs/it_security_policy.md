# IT Security Policy — Nimbus Telecom (Fictional)

## Passwords
Passwords must be at least 12 characters long and contain upper-case letters, lower-case letters, numbers and a special character. Passwords expire every 90 days and the last 5 passwords cannot be reused. Never share your password with anyone, including the IT helpdesk.

## Multi-Factor Authentication
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, VPN and all cloud applications. Use the company authenticator app; SMS codes are allowed only as a backup method.

## Lost or Stolen Devices
Report a lost or stolen laptop or phone to the IT helpdesk within 1 hour by calling extension 4357 or raising a P1 ticket. IT will remotely lock and wipe the device. A police complaint (FIR) copy is required for replacement of a stolen laptop.

## Phishing
Do not click links or open attachments in unexpected emails. Report suspicious emails using the "Report Phish" button in Outlook. The security team reviews reported emails within 4 hours.

## Software Installation
Only software from the approved Company Portal may be installed. Requests for new software are raised through a ticket and need manager plus IT security approval. Personal USB storage devices are blocked on all company laptops.

## Data Classification
Data is classified as Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. Customer personal data is always Restricted and must never be stored on personal devices or shared through personal email.
