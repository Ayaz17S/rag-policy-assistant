# Leave Policy — Nimbus Telecom (Fictional)

## Annual Leave
Every full-time employee receives 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues at 2 days per completed month of service. Up to 10 unused days can be carried forward to the next year; any balance above 10 days lapses on 31 March.

## Sick Leave
Employees get 12 days of paid sick leave per year. Sick leave cannot be carried forward. A medical certificate is required for sick leave longer than 2 consecutive days.

## Maternity and Paternity Leave
Maternity leave is 26 weeks of paid leave for the first two children. Paternity leave is 10 working days, which must be taken within 3 months of the child's birth or adoption.

## Applying for Leave
Leave requests must be submitted on the HR portal at least 7 days in advance for planned leave. The reporting manager approves or rejects the request within 2 working days. Unplanned leave must be informed to the manager by 10 AM on the same day.

## Leave Encashment
At the time of resignation, unused annual leave of up to 30 days is encashed based on basic salary. Sick leave is not encashable.
